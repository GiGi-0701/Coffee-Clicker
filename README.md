# ☕ Coffee Clicker

**Coffee Clicker** is an addictive incremental clicker game where you brew, upgrade, and expand your coffee empire one tap at a time! Track your Coffee Per Second (CPS), unlock upgrades, collect special currencies, and enjoy seasonal events — all while having fun with coffee-themed mechanics.  

---

## 🕹️ Features

- **Click & Earn:** Tap the big coffee button to earn coffee instantly.
- **Progression & Upgrades:** Buy coffee machines, mochas, coffee shops, and factories. Upgrade them to boost your CPS exponentially.
- **Economy System:** Earn coins by selling items or coffee and reinvest to grow faster.
- **Time Travel:** Skip 30 minutes into the future to collect coffee instantly.
- **Ascension System:** Reset progress for permanent multipliers and earn special currency called Pearls.
- **Special Events:** Enjoy Halloween, Christmas, Easter, and other seasonal events with unique items, mini-games, skins, and achievements.
- **Skins & Cosmetics:** Unlock 10+ unique skins with special currencies like cookies and event currencies.
- **Achievements:** Track your progress and earn rewards.
- **Customizable UI:** Pin favorite pages to the bottom navigation and toggle number formatting.

---

## 📜 Gameplay Overview

1. **Home Screen:**  
   - Display of coffee count (formatted as `3.4B`, `AA`, etc.).  
   - Click the big coffee to earn coffee.  
   - Bottom stats: CPS, coins, and event timer (500 seconds for bonus events).

2. **Store Page:**  
   - Buy 15 unique items (coffee machines, mochas, employees, etc.).  
   - Prices increase by 1.15× after each purchase.  
   - Each item contributes to CPS.

3. **Selling Page:**  
   - Sell items or coffee for coins (10% of the original price).  

4. **Upgrades Page:**  
   - Upgrade click power and items.  
   - Each upgrade increases click power or CPS with scaling cost multipliers.

5. **Skins Page:**  
   - Buy and equip skins using cookies (special currency).  

6. **Coffee Shop:**  
   - Convert coins into coffee at a 1:10 ratio.  

7. **Time Travel Page:**  
   - Buy clocks with coins to skip 30 minutes forward and collect coffee instantly.  

8. **Achievements Page:**  
   - Unlock achievements for milestones and item collection.  
   - Earn cookies and special event currencies.

9. **Settings Page:**  
   - Pin favorite pages, track stats, and toggle number formatting.  

10. **Ascension & Pearls:**  
    - Reset progress after reaching milestones to multiply item CPS and earn Pearls.  
    - Use Pearls in the Pearl Shop for permanent upgrades.
