# ☕ Coffee Clicker

Welcome to **Coffee Clicker**, the ultimate coffee empire simulator! Build your coffee empire by clicking, upgrading, selling, and ascending to reach new heights. Are you ready to brew the biggest coffee fortune ever?  

---

## 🎮 Overview

Coffee Clicker is an incremental/clicker game where you manage coffee production and expand your empire through clever upgrades, strategic selling, and powerful ascensions.  

**Core loop:**  
Click → Collect Coffee → Earn Dollars → Buy Items & Upgrades → Boost CPS → Ascend → Repeat!

---

## 🖥️ Pages & Features

### 1. Homepage
- Click the coffee button to earn **coffee**.
- View your **score**, **CPS (Coffee per Second)**, and **progress bar** for special events.
- Special event: When the progress bar fills, enjoy **30 seconds of 10× CPS and 15× click power**.

### 2. Store
- Buy items to increase your CPS.
- **15 unique items** with scaling prices: each purchase increases the price by **1.15×**.
- Items range from small boosters to massive CPS generators.

### 3. Selling
- Sell items or coffee for **in-game money**.
- Use steppers to choose the quantity you want to sell.
- Example: 100 coffee = $10, 1 item = proportional coffee value.

### 4. Upgrades
- Upgrade **cursor**: Click power ×4 (cost ×5 each upgrade).
- Upgrade **items**: Item CPS ×2 (cost ×5 each upgrade).
- Prices scale per upgrade, not per store item.

### 5. Coffee Shop
- Exchange **money** for coffee.
- Pricing example: $10 = 100 coffee, $100,000 = 1,000,000 coffee.

### 6. Skins
- Unlock cosmetic skins for milestone achievements: 1AA, 1AB, 1AC, etc.

### 7. Time Travel
- Buy **clocks** to fast-forward coffee production.
- 1 clock = 30 minutes of coffee. Prices scale massively (×1000 per purchase).

### 8. Settings & Stats
- Track your stats:
  - Total coffee made
  - Total spent
  - Items bought
  - Times ascended

### 9. Ascension
- Unlock after meeting requirements:  
  - 50 trillion coffee made  
  - 25 trillion coffee spent  
  - 5,000 items bought  
- Cost: 999 AA dollars (doubles each ascension)
- Rewards:
  - Earn **pearls** (special currency)
  - Double click power, CPS, and upgrade effects
  - Resets all progress except **skins** and **pearls**

### 10. Pearl Shop
- Spend pearls on **special upgrades**:
  - 50% cheaper items in the store
  - 50% cheaper upgrades
  - Items sell for 2× more coffee

---

## 💰 Currencies

1. **Coffee** – Main currency used for clicking and selling.  
2. **Dollars ($)** – Earned from selling coffee/items; used for purchases in the Coffee Shop.  
3. **Pearls** – Premium currency earned through ascension; used for permanent upgrades.

---

## 🚀 Progression

1. Click coffee to earn CPS.  
2. Buy items and upgrades to boost your CPS and click power.  
3. Reach milestones and special events for temporary multipliers.  
4. Ascend to earn pearls and unlock permanent multipliers.  
5. Expand your empire and dominate the coffee world!
